# From an album question to a verified chart result

## Music+ pre-stake review

Music+ adds bonded review to the existing Music implementation described below. Check [the release proof record](MUSICPLUS-WIP.md) and its dated manifest for actual deployment status. The original release remains accessible at `/music/previous`; its past entries and native payouts are preserved.

The new proposal form signs `list_market` on Challenges with a 0.004 simulated StudioNet GEN listing deposit. Validators compile the exact question, then activate the review window after finality. The app displays the compiled album, artist, Saturday chart date, edition policy and original proposal. The default review lasts 24 hours; the demonstration explicitly uses ten minutes. An entry cutoff must allow the full window plus one minute. Staking remains unavailable during review.

During that window, open **Challenge this specification**, select a ground and explain the issue. Wrong album/artist and wrong chart week require frozen release evidence: an allowlisted Archive.org `id_` snapshot or a Wikipedia `oldid` revision. Live pages are rejected. Edition-rule and outcome-bin disputes can cite the exact compiled specification. Each ground can be used once; one unresolved challenge blocks another. The wallet signs an exact 0.001 GEN bond.

Independent validators compare the frozen proposal with the archived Billboard reference and release evidence. They rerun the judgment, compare verdict and bounded option, and check retained quotations against their own fetches. UPHELD preserves the proposal. AMENDED applies only a code-generated week, edition policy or corrected-bin choice, then displays before/after specifications and both quotes. VOIDED permanently prevents staking and refunds all original deposits. Genuine uncertainty returns the bond; an expired decision is labelled a deadline fallback rather than a validator ruling.

The window must finish and every challenge must finalize before the approved hash can open. Opening and GEN pool creation are permissionless zero-GEN operations, performed by the app or keeper. The ticket then accepts the existing 0.01–100 GEN range stakes. Upcoming album outcomes remain undecided; historical tests remain excluded from competitive rankings. Reissues use an explicit chart-position/re-entry mode rather than claiming a second debut.

Unused listing credit unlocks when the reviewed market opens or its deadline expires. An amendment returns the bond plus a 0.001 GEN reward from the deposit; an upheld challenge assigns the bond to the creator. Void cases refund every original deposit. Choose **Collect review credit** and sign a zero-value withdrawal. A successful parent call is only a request: the app waits for independently verified native credit. Withdrawal status remains visible even at zero available credit. Verification locks another collection; an independently confirmed failed transfer restores the credit and exposes Retry review collection. A failed payable entry can also recover an exact credited deposit through its finalized receipt, without sending another deposit.

Run `npm run settle:music -- MARKET_ID` from the repository to resume review and settlement maintenance. The journal retains submitted hashes; rerunning does not blindly resend them. Resolution begins at the frozen observation time, waits for complete archive evidence, and voids at the fixed deadline if evidence remains missing. Pool refunds are available one day later. Exact upcoming dates and commands are in the release proof record. The owner uploads the recorded Music+ walkthrough and submits the Portal draft.

## Original release: compilation and chart resolution

The following sections explain the preserved v1 contracts and their historical proofs. Their original `compile` and `open_market` methods are contract reference; the current proposal UI uses the Music+ listing and review flow above.

Bullseye Music extends the Film programme with Billboard 200 album debut markets. It does not use Hot 100 song rankings. The app runs on StudioNet, chain 61999, whose native GEN is simulated development currency. Historical examples disclose that their outcomes are already public and contribute to exhibition standings rather than competitive rankings. This tutorial follows the actual implementation in `contracts/music_market_logic.py`, the generated standalone `music_markets.py`, `music_rows.py`, `music_pools.py`, and `seasons.py`.

## Write a question, then review the compilation

Visit `/music/propose`. A creator supplies a plain-English question, an entry cutoff, a resolution deadline, and the historical-practice flag. For example: “Where did Renaissance by Beyoncé debut on the Billboard 200 chart dated August 13, 2022?” The wallet submits `compile`. It supplies no authoritative album specification, chart position, winning range, or archive URL.

Validators independently interpret the question with one bounded model call. The fixed response schema contains a decision, market type, albums, and a rejection reason. Accepted album identities contain the title, primary artist credit, explicitly named alternate-edition titles, exact Saturday chart date, and one canonical identity rule. `combined_standard_deluxe` means the named editions count under the standard Billboard entry; `exact_edition` requires the explicitly separate edition. Collaborative primary credits remain part of identity. Another artist's album with the same title cannot stand in for the named artist.

A question lacking an exact title or date is rejected instead of guessed. A rejection has a null market type, an empty album array, and a reason. Independent validators must agree on the decision; accepted specifications must also agree in their complete canonical content. Rejection reasons may use different wording because they do not define a market. The application presents the reason so the creator can revise the question.

For accepted questions, code assigns the fixed six rank bins or the two head-to-head choices, derives the observation time from the chart dates, and freezes the deadlines and snapshot policy. Competitive entries must close no later than 00:00 UTC fifteen days before the Saturday chart date, conservatively before its Friday–Thursday tracking week. For example, Billboard dates the Midnights debut November 5, 2022 after its October 21–27 tracking week. The canonical specification is hashed. A protected self callback activates the compilation only after the network finalizes its transaction. The creator reviews this specification and submits `open_market` with its hash. A second finality callback opens entries. The review step protects creators from opening a different interpretation of their question.

## Pick an outcome

The market page shows the album, artist, chart week, outcomes, and one GEN ticket. An entry can stake 0.01–100 StudioNet GEN. The wallet signs the actual native-value contract call; entering a number or choosing a range alone transfers nothing. The no-model pool contract reads the finalized open source, copies its immutable hash and deadline, and creates one shared pool per music market. The pool stakes are distinct from the optional free-points prediction. Free entries may include an exact rank, which must fit the selected bin.

The browser saves public transaction metadata per wallet. Submitted, pending, accepted, finalized-success, and finalized-error are different states. Acceptance is provisional, and a network appeal may still affect the result before finality. Reloading restores tracking. A successful parent claim is not presented as received GEN until its linked native transfer has independently finalized with explicit value credit.

## Fetch archived evidence before interpreting identity

After entries close and the chart observation time arrives, anyone can request adjudication. Validators query the Wayback CDX index for the fixed dated Billboard 200 URL. They consider at most the first eight chronological successful captures between observation and the transaction's frozen observation timestamp. A participant cannot nominate a favorable capture. Each capture uses the `id_` form to retrieve the archived publisher HTML.

Code first validates the chart date picker and Billboard 200 identity. Then an HTML parser extracts exactly 200 ordered, unique positions with title, artist credit, last-week position, and weeks on chart. Both modern labeled cells and the older duplicated mobile statistics format are supported. Partial pages, missing positions, extra chart rows, another chart week, and Hot 100 markers are ineligible. Advertisements and page scripts are not sent to the model.

This matters in the actual Renaissance proof: an earlier capture contained only 37 rows, so the validators selected the later complete October 19, 2022 capture. The entire canonical row array receives a SHA-256 digest. Only these extracted rows and the frozen specification enter the resolution prompt. The model identifies the intended album or reports that it is absent. A matched row must have one week on chart to establish a debut. Its returned quote must exactly match code's canonical quote at that position.

Independent validators must agree on capture metadata, the full rows digest, and matched positions. The leader's quote must occur in each validator's own extraction. Matching only the winning bin is insufficient. Code assigns the rank bin or compares head-to-head positions. One absent album loses to a charted album; equal positions or both absent void the pairing. Missing or genuinely ambiguous evidence remains pending until the fixed resolution deadline, then voids. Protected finality callbacks activate resolved or void outcomes.

## Settle, collect, and build a record

The live third rank proof uses `Patina` by Tallies on August 13, 2022, testing absence from a complete chart. The artist's [release page](https://tallies.bandcamp.com/album/patina) dates the album to July 29, 2022. Its outcome still requires independent validator extraction and judgment; the release page does not determine a Billboard position. The older Midnights deluxe proposal remains a separate diagnostic: it compiled successfully, but two finalized adjudications found no qualifying archive evidence. It stays pending rather than receiving a manually supplied rank. The retained premature season import failed as intended because pending results cannot earn points.

The first Patina adjudication exposed another real JSON omission: an absent entry returned only an empty quote, omitting `position:null`. A finalized zero-GEN diagnostic preserved this raw response. The corrected interpreter canonicalizes only that exact one-key, empty-quote object after complete extraction. A quoted row missing its position, an object with extra fields, or a partial chart still cannot pass. The three replacement contracts rerun all positive cases on-chain; the superseded candidate's successful 0.01 GEN payout remains separate, fully collected proof history.

`BullseyeMusicPools` has no LLM. It settles only from a finalized source result with the same frozen specification. Winners share the whole pot proportionally, without a fee. Cumulative integer division allocates all rounding remainders deterministically. A void market or an empty winning bin refunds original stakes. A source that remains unresolved can be refunded at its resolution deadline plus one day. These are permissionless paths, not administrator awards.

Claims reserve an amount under a nonce before emitting a native transfer. Verification checks the finalized successful claim parent, its exact calldata, its linked finalized native child, sender, recipient, amount, and explicit credit. A verified failed transfer restores retry availability under a new nonce. Pending or unknown credit stays reserved to prevent duplicate payments. A missed settlement callback can be recovered using its finalized protocol receipt. Native finality still depends on StudioNet completing its messages; the contract cannot safely pretend an unknown transfer failed.

Finally, `BullseyeSeasons` imports existing finalized source predictions from Film and Music. A correct bin earns 100 points. Optional exact Film guesses reuse the 100-at-exact curve reaching zero at ±25%; rank closeness decreases by position distance across 200 places. Records are idempotent, chronological streaks are reproducible, and void results do not count toward accuracy. Historical results have a separate exhibition table. The season page combines both categories' recent results and the connected wallet's free-points track record. GEN pool positions remain on their market pages.

See the dated manifest in `docs/proofs/music-2026-10-12-release-v2/manifest.json` for actual addresses, receipts, and current completion status. `npm run verify:music` performs read-only network checks and deliberately fails if a required live proof, owner-approved stake, native claim, or source commit is missing. Controlled browser wallet fixtures exercise UX but never count as a live funded proof.

Tracking-week provenance: [Billboard reporting on The Tortured Poets Department](https://ca.billboard.com/music/chart-beat/taylor-swifts-the-tortured-poets-department-eclipses-midnights-streaming-record-in-four-days) relates the April 19–25 tracking activity to the May 4, 2024 chart date. The fixed fifteen-day cutoff is code policy derived from that dated reporting.

### A live formatting failure and its bounded correction

The StudioNet compiler diagnostic returned a rejected decision, an empty album list and a useful missing-information reason, but omitted the null-valued `market_type` field. The contract now restores that null only for this otherwise exact three-field rejection. It does not infer an album, artist, date or accepted market type. Accepted proposals still require the complete schema, and independent validators still compile the question themselves. Both a regression test for the observed rejection and a test forbidding this normalization on accepted markets run in direct mode. The shipping replacement deployment reran the rejection and positive cases. Early superseded candidates received no GEN; the later funded candidate received exactly 0.01 GEN and credited it back in full before the absence correction, as its manifest discloses.
