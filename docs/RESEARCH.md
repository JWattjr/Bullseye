# Research limits

Official [GenLayer developer documentation](https://docs.genlayer.com/developers) and the installed SDK were used for contract, equivalence-principle, finality and client integration. Actual validator probes and finalized receipts determine what this MVP claims to support; ordinary browser retrieval does not establish validator accessibility.

The saved Claude review's Portal points ranges were treated as hypotheses. The [Portal landing page](https://dev.points.genlayer.foundation/) describes contribution tracks, but does not establish the proposed app/repository/tutorial award ranges or this project's eligibility. No authoritative rubric for those specific ranges was verified during the October 1, 2026 research. They are omitted from the repository and submission drafts; no additive reward total or award promise is made. The [ecosystem page](https://genlayer.com/ecosystem) provides a Portal entry point, not an eligibility determination.
